# Attribution and scope

This is an independent research/learning project, not an official release or endorsement by the upstream authors.

- [AutoBio](https://github.com/autobio-bench/AutoBio) supplies the simulated task and demonstrations. The repository records its pinned revision and two compatibility edits.
- [WorldGym](https://github.com/world-model-eval/world-model-eval) supplies the action-conditioned video world model. [World-Gymnast](https://world-gymnast.github.io/) is the method reference for the native RL exploration.
- [LeRobot](https://github.com/huggingface/lerobot) supplies ACT and SmolVLA. The runtime also uses the original SmolVLM, Qwen2.5-VL and SD3 VAE assets identified in Scripts/configs/assets.json.
- The learning structure is inspired by [classifier-notebooks-undergraduate](https://github.com/Kevin11Kaikai/classifier-notebooks-undergraduate/tree/main). Its notebook code/prose is not copied here.
- The three images in media/figures are AI-generated teaching schematics, not experimental screenshots. Videos and numerical tables are saved simulation evidence. Authoring prompts and duplicate exports are not bundled.
- Scripts/SOURCE_PROVENANCE.json records the original local implementation sources and portability changes. Official algorithms are not claimed as inventions of this project.

Upstream code, datasets and public model weights are fetched separately and remain subject to their own terms. Locally trained adapters are external assets and are not currently published. This project does not apply a blanket license to third-party code, weights or media; attribution does not replace the relevant rightsholders' terms.
